## Goal

Quasi-stationary heat period selection for expert assessment (CSN 730540-2, section 3)

## Requirements

### R1: Quasi-stationary window selection

Select periods of >= 3 consecutive warm days (tmax >= 26, tmin >= 13) whose **outdoor daily
maxima stay <= 30.0 degC** (the CSN 73 0540-3 design-day peak, Human-confirmed criterion) from
the public parquet, outdoor reference Atrea `temp_ambient` sm2_01.

**Acceptance Criterion:** `analysis/window_summary.csv` contains exactly 8 windows (2024-2026),
including `2024-08-02..2024-08-04` and `2026-07-08..2026-07-11`.

### R2: Frozen-weekend handling

Days whose hourly outdoor series has std < 0.1 degC with >= 12 readings are carried-over weekend
values ("frozen"); they are flagged, excluded from window day-counting (windows need >= 3 real
days) and shaded in the graphs. Logged as `_grid4d/issue/atrea-weekend-frozen-readings.md`.

**Acceptance Criterion:** `analysis/outdoor_daily_classification.csv` has a `frozen` column with
True values; every window in `window_summary.csv` has `real_days >= 3`.

### R3: Indoor evaluation against 27 / 30 degC limits

Per section (Atrea extracted air) and per room (ThermoPro 1NP/5NP): daily min/mean/max, share of
hours above 27 / 30 degC, days with indoor daily max > 30 degC, night minimum. Decisive windows
(outdoor <= 30 while indoor > 30): 2024-08-02..04 (3/9 sections), 2025-08-19..21 and
2026-07-08..11 (room 5NP-S3, 30.9 / 31.1 degC).

**Acceptance Criterion:** `analysis/indoor_sections_2024-08-02_2024-08-04.csv` shows 3 sections
with `h_gt30 > 0`; `analysis/thermopro_rooms_2026-07-08_2026-07-11.csv` shows room `5NP-S3` with
`tmax` 31.1.

### R4: Expert-ready deliverables

English report with method, results and caveats; hourly CSV exports and window graphs for every
window; Czech reply draft for the expert kept outside git (`_inbox/dwh-sm2/`, repo is public).

**Acceptance Criterion:** `analysis/DWH-SM2-0002-report.md` exists and documents method, the
three decisive windows, and the caveats (extracted-air semantics, air vs operative temperature,
frozen weekends, ThermoPro coverage).

### R5: Candidate evidence table (Human-refined source semantics)

ThermoPro sensors sit on the floor CORRIDORS (1NP/5NP) - real measured temperatures with
practically no internal gains, focus floor: 5NP. Atrea `temp_indoor` is the AVERAGE
extracted-air temperature across ALL flats of a section (1-9), so a section average above the
limit is a lower bound - the critical flats were hotter. Collect every candidate location whose
indoor maximum exceeds the 30 degC air limit inside a selected window, from both sources:
measured 5NP corridor sensors (ThermoPro) and section averages (Atrea).

**Acceptance Criterion:** `analysis/candidates.csv` lists 3 Atrea section rows for
`2024-08-02..2024-08-04` (sm2_01/sm2_04/sm2_08) and measured corridor sensor `5NP-S3` for both
ThermoPro windows (30.9 / 31.1 degC).
